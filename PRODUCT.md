# Product

## Product Name

Kadra

## Register

product

## Users

Kadra is for people who want a personal, focused place to keep up with their fixed-income investments from a mobile-first interface. Users check their portfolio briefly and repeatedly to understand current value, estimated income, projected growth, upcoming maturities, and what deserves attention next.

## Product Purpose

Kadra helps users see what they own, what it is worth, what it is earning, how it may grow, and what deserves attention. Success means someone can open the app for a quick check-in and leave with a clearer understanding of their portfolio.

## Product Boundary

Kadra records and models investment information entered by the user. It does not connect to financial institutions, move money, execute trades, or require an account. Local storage and user-controlled backups support the experience without defining its personality.

## Brand Personality

Kadra should feel calm, clear, personal, precise, curious, rewarding, and quietly playful. Financial information should remain dependable and easy to scan, while discovery, navigation, progress, and other reversible interactions can carry more personality and delight.

## Anti-references

Kadra should not feel like a generic SaaS dashboard, crypto trading interface, loud marketing site, or promotion-heavy consumer banking app. Avoid noisy charts, speculative urgency, gambling-like rewards, and interface patterns that compete with the user's financial data. These anti-references should prevent hype, not force the product into austerity.

## Design Principles

- Prioritize fast comprehension over decorative complexity.
- Make financial numbers prominent, readable, and dependable.
- Keep actions predictable and familiar across screens.
- Keep financial data visually stable while allowing stateful interactions to feel tactile and expressive.
- Use personality selectively in search, navigation, progress, empty states, and other low-risk moments.
- Preserve local-first product behavior: storage, backups, and data ownership should feel intentional and understandable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Kadra should target WCAG AA as a practical accessibility baseline. Text contrast, focus states, form labels, destructive confirmations, touch targets, and reduced-motion behavior should be treated as product quality requirements, especially for mobile use.
